# Accessible image text

Choose alternative text from the image's purpose, never from its filename, internal code, or visual caption.

- **Informative:** describe the information needed in the surrounding context, briefly and in that page's language.
- **Functional:** when an image is the only content of a link or button, describe the destination or action.
- **Decorative or redundant:** use `alt=""`, including images inside an already named link or button when they add no information.
- **Text in an image:** include the meaningful visible words unless the same words are already available next to it.

Visual captions and `alt` text are separate editorial fields. A caption may be playful; the alternative must remain equivalent and useful.
